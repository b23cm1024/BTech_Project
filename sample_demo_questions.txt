4. CATEGORY: MEDICAL EMERGENCY & SEMESTER-WIDE WITHDRAWAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Question:
Can a student withdraw from an entire semester if hospitalized for a month?

What it showcases:
- Intent disambiguation and natural language tolerance.

- Procedural path: Requires formal recommendation by Convener DUGC (for UG) or DRC (for PG) and approval of DOAA.
